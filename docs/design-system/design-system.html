Graphite Glass

The v1 system keeps the app's Liquid Glass look — floating glass panels, capsule controls, a sliding tab pill over a graphite backdrop — and replaces 46 loosely named variables, 17 hard-coded hex colors and about 30 inline rgba() values with three token layers. Every text and glyph pairing is composited the way it renders and checked against WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes.

TokensThree layers

ref primitives (gray + 9 hues) → semantic roles per theme (surface, text, accent, status, agent state, meter, diff) → component specs. Components never use primitives directly.

AAAll pairs pass

Current UI: 20 of 38 audited pairs fail AA (white on the blue gradient 2.86:1, tertiary text 3.50:1, blue/red/purple pills, focus ring 1.77:1). v1: every checked pair passes in dark and light.

ScaleFewer, named steps

12 font sizes → 7 roles · 25 spacing values → 12 · 14 radii → 7 · ad-hoc shadows → 5 levels · 5 blurs → 3 · 10 durations → 7 named.

One of eachMerged components

One segmented control (was .seg, .segc, .radios), one button with sizes, one menu (was .amenu, .pop, .ctxmenu), one pill with tones, one meter whose colors agree everywhere.

ThemesDark, Light, System

Dark stays the default and looks the same. Light is new. Reduce transparency and Reduce motion work in both.

한/ENMixed-language copy

The Korean UI keeps English product terms (Worker, Dispatcher, PR). Body text is 1.5 line height, Hangul doesn't break mid-word (word-break: keep-all), and nothing is smaller than 11px.

Color

Primitives are Apple system hues in five steps: 300 text on dark, 400 vivid on dark, 500 vivid on light, 600 solid fill under white text, 700 text on light. The dark 400s moved by at most 16/255 per channel from the current values so glyphs keep 3:1 on selected glass rows.

Semantic roles

Glass roles are shown over the canvas. Text roles show “Aa” on the canvas. Values follow the theme switch above.

Contrast — WCAG 2.2 AA

Each pair is composited the way it renders (glass alpha over the canvas, with and without the top wash and panel sheen), and the worst case counts. Text needs 4.5:1, glyphs and control edges 3:1. The table shows the theme selected above.

Typography

System fonts only (decision 30): SF Pro / Segoe UI Variable for Latin, Apple SD Gothic Neo / Malgun Gothic for Hangul, in one stack. Sizes follow the text-size setting through --px; try the stepper at the top. Weights have roles: 400 body, 500 controls and row titles, 600 labels and headers, 700 only for view and page titles.

Mixed Korean and English

Line height

--lh-ui 1.4 for single-line controls and rows, --lh-body 1.5 for messages, cards and descriptions. Hangul has taller glyphs than Latin at the same size; 1.38 (current body) crowds two-line Korean bubbles.

Breaking

word-break: keep-all under :lang(ko) keeps 어절 whole; overflow-wrap: anywhere still breaks long paths and URLs. Set lang on <html> from the language setting (already done).

Terms

Product terms stay English in Korean copy: Worker, Dispatcher, PR, Claude Code. Particles attach directly: “W-02는”, “Worker에게”. No letter-spacing on Hangul; -0.01em only on titles 17px and up.

Numbers

Counts, percentages, times and token sizes use tabular-nums so meters and badges don't jitter as values change.

Space, radius, size

A 4px grid with 2, 6 and 10 as half steps. Control heights come in three sizes, and hit targets are at least 24×24 (WCAG 2.5.8): the current 16px chip remove button and 18px clear button grow their target with a transparent inset.

Spacing

Radius

Nested corners stay concentric: inner radius ≈ outer radius − padding. Panel 20 with 10 padding → rows 10; card 14 with 6 padding → menu items 8.

Elevation and glass

Depth comes from three things: a specular rim (light from the upper left), a drop shadow level, and blur for anything that floats over content. In light theme the rim becomes a white highlight plus a dark hairline.

Motion

Seven durations and five curves. Color and background changes use --dur-base with --ease-standard; reveals use --dur-fast; presses use --dur-instant. The tab pill keeps its signature move — a spring slide with a squash-and-stretch — on --dur-glide. With Reduce motion every duration becomes 0 and looping animations stop.

Tab pill (glide)transform/width: --dur-glide --ease-spring · stretch: --ease-stretch

Icons

Line icons on a 20×20 grid with a 1.6 stroke, round caps and joins, drawn in currentColor. Status glyphs use a 16 grid and always sit next to a text label, so state never depends on color alone. v1 fixes the duplicate s-back id (two symbols, 8×12 and 16×16) and normalizes the six stroke widths now in use (1.5–2.4) to 1.6, except the send arrow (2.2) and gear spokes (2.4), which render at 13–16px.
